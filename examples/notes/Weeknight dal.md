# Weeknight dal

Serves 3, about 35 minutes.

- 200 g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, 3 cloves garlic, a thumb of ginger
- 1 tsp each: cumin seeds, turmeric, ground coriander
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- Lemon and coriander to finish

Fry the cumin seeds in oil until they crackle, then the onion until soft, then the garlic and ginger. Stir in the ground spices for 30 seconds. Add the lentils, tomatoes and 700 ml water. Simmer for 20–25 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick. Season, add lemon, and top with coriander.

Notes to self: it's better the next day. Double the ginger if anyone has a cold.
