# Bike maintenance

## Tyres

Commuter: 32 mm tyres at 4.5 bar front, 5 bar rear. Check every Sunday; they lose about 0.5 bar a week.

## Chain

Clean and re-lube every 300 km, or after any ride in the rain. Measure wear every 1,000 km; replace the chain at 0.5% stretch, before it starts eating the cassette.

## Brakes

The rear pads were replaced in March. The front pads are at about half, so check them again in September.
