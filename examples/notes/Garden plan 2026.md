# Garden plan 2026

## Raised beds

Two beds, 1.2 m by 2.4 m, along the south fence. Bed one: tomatoes (San Marzano and a cherry variety), basil between them. Bed two: courgettes at the far end, then rows of chard and spinach.

## Timing

Start tomatoes indoors in mid-March. Plant out after the last frost, usually around 10 May here. Courgettes go straight in at the end of May.

## Lessons from last year

Watering by hand in July was the bottleneck. This year: a soaker hose on a timer, 20 minutes at 6 am. Netting over the chard went up too late and the pigeons got it.
