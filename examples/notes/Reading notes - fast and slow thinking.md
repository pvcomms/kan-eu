# Reading notes: fast and slow thinking

My own summary, from memory, of the two-systems idea in Kahneman's book.

## Two modes

The fast mode is automatic: it reads a face, finishes "bread and…", and recoils from a sudden noise. The slow mode is effortful: long division, checking an argument, filling in a tax form. The fast mode is always on and hands its impressions to the slow one, which mostly endorses them.

## What I took from it

Most bad decisions I've made weren't slow-mode failures. They were fast-mode answers that the slow mode never checked. The fix isn't to think slowly about everything; it's to know which questions deserve the slow pass.
